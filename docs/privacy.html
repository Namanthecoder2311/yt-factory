Privacy Policy

Last updated: 1 November 2026

yt-factory ("the app") is an internal tool operated by the owner of the YouTube channels Midnight Aisle Tales and Wigglo Toons. It is used only by the owner, on the owner's own YouTube channels.

What the app accesses

With the owner's authorization (Google OAuth), the app uses the YouTube Data API to:

  • read the authorized channel's basic information (channel title and handle), to confirm it uploads to the correct channel;
  • upload the owner's own videos and set their title, description, tags, thumbnail, audience setting and scheduled publish time.

What the app does not do

  • It does not access, collect or store data about any viewer or other YouTube user.
  • It does not read comments, messages, subscribers or analytics of other channels.
  • It does not sell, share or transfer any data to third parties.
  • It does not use YouTube data for advertising.

Storage and security

The OAuth refresh token is stored only as an encrypted secret in the owner's private GitHub repository and is used only by the owner's automated workflows. No other personal data is stored.

Children

Videos intended for children are marked "Made for Kids" in line with COPPA and YouTube policies. The app collects no data from children or anyone else.

Google and YouTube

The app uses YouTube API Services. See the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy. Access can be revoked at any time at Google security settings.
