## Winning formula (sab channels mein common)

1. **Title:** `[Number] [Jagah ya situation] Horror Stories Animated`
   - Jagah/situation jo baar-baar hit hui: **Walmart, School Lockdown, Amazon Delivery, Airbnb, Snapchat, Field Trip, Hitchhiking, Bus Stop, Pool, House Sitting, Home Invasion, Camping at Night, Driving at Night, Home Alone, Netflix and Chill, Christmas, Thunderstorm, Ghost Hunting**
   - Har koi relatable jagah hai, jahan viewer khud ja chuka hai, aur wahan darr lagna "mere saath bhi ho sakta hai" wali feeling deta hai.
2. **Length:** single videos 18–25 min (3 stories), aur mahine mein ek baar **2–3.5 ghante ki compilation** (sab stories jod ke). Compilations par views sabse zyada aur sabse lambe samay tak aate rehte hain.
3. **List formula:** outliers mein sabse common formula "The List" hai (71 outliers).
4. **Shorts:** isme sample nahi kiya, agle research mein karenge.

## Hum kya copy karenge, kya nahi

✅ **Copy karenge (yeh allowed hai):**
- Title ka pattern aur situations ki list
- Video ka structure (3 stories, ~20 min, monthly mega-compilation)
- Upload rhythm, thumbnail ka style (bada text + ek darr wala moment)

❌ **Copy nahi karenge (copyright strike ya channel ban ka risk):**
- Unki stories, script, artwork, characters, voice ya clips
- Unke videos ko reupload ya "re-voice" karna, jo YouTube ki **reused/inauthentic content** policy mein aata hai
- "TRUE" tabhi likhenge jab story sach mein kisi ne bheji ho (baad mein subscriber submissions se). Abhi titles mein "TRUE" nahi likhenge.
